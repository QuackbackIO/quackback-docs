# Support

Customers reach out two ways: a quick question they want answered now, or a request that needs tracking until it's actually done. Quackback handles both without asking your team to juggle two tools.

**Conversations** are real-time chats. A visitor opens Messenger in your widget, or a support email lands in your inbox, and you reply back and forth like a chat thread. **Tickets** are durable, trackable requests with a customer-visible progress stage, for anything that outlives a single conversation.

Both flow into one place: **Admin → Inbox**. A conversation can turn into a ticket without losing its history, and a ticket stays linked back to the conversation it came from.

# Set SLA policies

Response-time promises only matter if you can see when you're about to miss one. SLA policies set targets for how fast your team responds and resolves, and surface a countdown before you breach them.

Configure named policies at **Admin → Settings → SLA policies**.

## Targets

A policy can define up to three targets:

- **First response**: time to the first reply
- **Next response**: time to reply while the customer is waiting on you again
- **Time to close**: time to fully resolve the conversation

Leave any target blank to skip tracking it.

## Office-hours aware

Deadlines are calculated against your [office hours](/support/office-hours), so a target doesn't silently burn down overnight or over a weekend. Pin a specific schedule to a policy, or leave it unset to use your workspace's default schedule. A policy with no schedule configured anywhere is treated as always-on, 24/7.

## Pause while snoozed

Turn on **Pause while snoozed** (on by default) so a policy's clocks stop counting down while the conversation is snoozed, and pick back up from where they left off once it wakes.

## Apply through a workflow, never automatically

SLA policies don't match themselves to conversations. Apply one with the **Apply SLA** action inside a [workflow](/automation/workflows), for example based on priority or which board or team a conversation belongs to. A conversation has at most one active SLA at a time. Applying a new one replaces the old, and an agent can remove an applied SLA directly from the conversation if it no longer fits.

<Tip>
Build a workflow that applies your "Urgent" SLA policy whenever priority is set to Urgent, so your fastest targets kick in the moment a conversation needs them, without anyone remembering to set it by hand.
</Tip>

## Breaches feed your automation

Approaching and actual breaches both fire events your workflows can act on, whether that's alerting a team, reassigning the conversation, or sending a webhook to an external system. See [Build a workflow](/automation/workflows) for the full trigger list.

## Archive and clone

Archive a policy to stop it from being applied to new conversations without losing history on the conversations that already have it. Clone a policy to start a new one from an existing set of targets instead of building from scratch.

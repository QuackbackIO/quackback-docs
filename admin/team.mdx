# Manage your team

Don't manage feedback alone. Invite teammates to help triage requests, respond to users, and keep your roadmap updated. Control who can do what with simple role-based permissions.

## Overview

Access team settings from **Admin → Settings → Members & Teams** (`/admin/settings/members`). The page has three tabs: **Members**, **Teams**, and **Roles**.

<Note>
The old Team Members page now redirects here. Any bookmarked links to the previous URL land on the Members tab automatically.
</Note>

## Roles

Every teammate has a role that determines what they can configure. Quackback ships four role presets, from broadest to narrowest access: **Owner**, **Admin**, **Manager**, and **Contributor**.

For the full breakdown of what each role can do, see [Understand roles and permissions](/admin/roles-permissions). The **Roles** tab in Members & Teams shows the same permission catalogue read-only, so you can check exactly what a role grants before assigning it.

## Invite team members

1. Go to **Admin → Settings → Members & Teams**
2. Click **"Invite member"**
3. Enter their email address
4. Select a role
5. Click **Send invite**

The invitee receives an email with a link to join your workspace.

### Pending Invitations

Pending invitations are shown at the top of the Members tab. From there you can:
- View who has been invited
- Resend invitations
- Cancel pending invitations

## Manage members

### View Team Members

The team page shows:
- Member name and email
- Role badge

The member count is displayed at the top of the list.

### Change Role

1. Find the member in the list
2. Click the **...** menu button on the right
3. Select a new role
4. Confirm the change

Changes take effect immediately.

### Remove Member

1. Find the member in the list
2. Click the **...** menu button
3. Select **Remove from team**
4. Confirm removal

Removed members:
- Are converted to a portal user
- Lose access to the admin dashboard
- Can still interact with the public feedback portal
- Their comments and activity history remain

<Warning>
You cannot remove yourself, and every workspace needs at least one Owner.
</Warning>

## Teams

Group members into teams to mirror how your organization is actually structured, like "Support" or "Product". Create and manage teams from the **Teams** tab in Members & Teams.

Teams do more than group people. You can assign conversations and tickets directly to a team, and use a team as the target for routing rules so incoming work lands with the right group automatically. Each team can distribute assigned work to its members manually, round-robin, or load-balanced across whoever's online.

<Tip>
Set up teams before you turn on routing rules. Routing an inbox to a team that doesn't exist yet means someone has to reassign everything by hand.
</Tip>

## Authentication

By default, team members sign in with a password or a [magic link](/auth/magic-link). You can also enable GitHub and Google sign-in, require [two-factor authentication](/admin/security#two-factor-authentication), or connect [single sign-on](/auth/sso).

See [Configure team security](/admin/security) for the full set of options.

## Best Practices

### Start Small

<Tip>
Begin with a small number of Owners and Admins. Add Managers and Contributors as your feedback volume grows.
</Tip>

### Clear Ownership

Assign specific team members to monitor certain boards or topics.

### Regular Reviews

Periodically review team access:
- Remove members who no longer need access
- Audit admin permissions
- Check for unused invitations

### Grant the Right Role

<Note>
Reserve Owner and Admin for people who need full workspace control. Manager and Contributor cover most day-to-day feedback and support work - see [Understand roles and permissions](/admin/roles-permissions) for the full breakdown.
</Note>

# Finish setup with the launch checklist

Setting up a new tool can feel like a scavenger hunt. The launch checklist tells you exactly what's left, tailored to what you're actually trying to do with Quackback.

<Note>
Getting your instance running for the first time? [Quick Start](/getting-started/quick-start) covers the Docker setup that comes before this checklist.
</Note>

## Pick your goal

During onboarding, you choose what you're primarily using Quackback for. This determines which checklist tasks you see.

| Goal | Best for |
|------|----------|
| **Product feedback** | Product teams collecting feature requests and bug reports |
| **Customer support** | Support teams handling conversations and tickets |
| **Help Center** | Support and content teams publishing self-serve articles |
| **Internal feedback** | Teams collecting feedback internally, without external users |

<Note>
Changed your mind? Click **Change goal** on the getting started page (**Admin → Getting started**) to pick a different outcome. Your checklist updates immediately.
</Note>

## Work through your tasks

Tasks are grouped into three categories:

- **Set up the essentials** - the prerequisites you need to get real value from Quackback, like creating a board or installing the widget.
- **Make it yours** - polish tasks like adding your logo or connecting an integration.
- **Next milestone** - the first-win task that proves setup worked, like receiving your first post or conversation. It completes automatically when the milestone happens - there's nothing to check off by hand.

### Skip a task

Not every task applies to you right now.

- Essentials show **Do later**, which moves them out of the way without losing them. Click **Move up** to bring one back.
- Polish tasks show **Skip**. Click **Add back** to restore a skipped task.
- The milestone task can't be skipped - it completes on its own when it happens.

## Tasks by goal

| Goal | Tasks |
|------|-------|
| Product feedback | Create a board, install the widget, invite a teammate, add your logo, connect an integration, receive your first post or vote |
| Customer support | Install Messenger, configure Messenger, invite a teammate, add your logo, connect an integration, receive your first conversation |
| Help Center | Prepare your first article, invite a teammate, add your logo, publish your first article |
| Internal feedback | Create a private team board, invite a teammate, add your logo, collect your first team idea |

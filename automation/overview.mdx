# AI & Automation

Support doesn't scale by hiring alone. Quackback pairs an AI teammate named Quinn with a trigger-driven workflows engine, so routine replies and routing happen without a human in the loop every time.

Everything lives in **Admin → Settings**, under **AI & Automation**: Agent, Copilot, Skills, Connectors, and Workflows.

<Note>
Quinn needs an [AI model configured](/admin/ai-features). The Agent replies in Messenger once the [support inbox](/support/overview) is on. Workflows appear when the support inbox is on.
</Note>

## Quinn: two peer agents

Quinn isn't one bot with two names. It's two separate configurations that share the same identity:

- **[Agent](/automation/quinn-agent)** replies to customers directly in the [Messenger](/widget) widget. It works the moment you deploy it.
- **[Copilot](/automation/quinn-copilot)** sits beside your team in the inbox: it answers questions, drafts replies, and never speaks to a customer directly.

Both agents draw on the same [knowledge sources](/automation/knowledge-sources), though what each can see differs. Both can run [actions](/automation/actions), including tools from MCP servers you add as connectors.

## Workflows

[Workflows](/automation/workflows) automate the parts of support that don't need judgment: routing a new conversation to the right team, applying an SLA by priority, closing a ticket after a week of silence. Workflows are built from triggers, conditions, and actions, and they work on both conversations and tickets.

Workflows ship with a [template gallery](/automation/templates) so you don't have to start from a blank canvas, and a library of reusable [conversational blocks](/automation/workflow-blocks) for building interactive Messenger flows.

## Monitor

Once Quinn is live, the **Quinn** section of **Admin → Analytics** shows resolution rates, handoffs, action outcomes, and how much your team uses Copilot. See [Monitor Quinn](/automation/test-and-monitor).

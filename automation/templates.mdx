# Start from a workflow template

Most workflows you need already exist. The template gallery gives you a working starting point you can rename, adjust, and publish, instead of building routing logic from scratch.

Find it from **Admin → AI & Automation → Workflows → New workflow**.

## Categories

| Category | Use it for |
|----------|-----------|
| **Popular** | The templates most workspaces start with |
| **Customer facing** | Guided Messenger conversations, like a triage bot or a VIP lane |
| **Routing** | Getting conversations to the right team or tag |
| **SLA & priority** | Applying and escalating around response-time commitments |
| **Housekeeping** | Cleanup work like closing idle conversations |

A few examples from the gallery:

- **Route conversations to the right team** -- Popular, Routing
- **Route by issue type** -- Popular, Routing
- **Apply SLAs by priority** -- Popular, SLA & priority
- **Escalate frustrated customers** -- Popular, SLA & priority
- **Escalate long waits** -- SLA & priority, Housekeeping
- **Auto-close idle conversations** -- Housekeeping
- **Post-resolution follow-up** -- Housekeeping
- **Tag billing keywords** -- Routing, Housekeeping
- **Front-door triage bot** -- Popular, Customer facing
- **VIP concierge lane** -- Customer facing
- **AI-first support with honest escalation** -- Customer facing

<Tip>
Start with a Popular template even if your use case seems specific. They're the most tested starting points, and you can still customize every node afterward.
</Tip>

## Engagement funnels

Templates tagged Customer facing run a guided conversation with [workflow blocks](/automation/workflow-blocks), so their reporting includes an engagement funnel: how many runs were **sent**, how many the customer **engaged** with, and how many **completed**. Use it to spot a flow that's sending fine but losing customers partway through.

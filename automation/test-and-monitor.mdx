# Monitor Quinn

Once Quinn is live, keep an eye on how it's actually doing. Quinn's numbers live with the rest of your reporting.

Find them at **Admin → Analytics**, in the **Quinn** section. Pick a time period at the top, the same as the other [analytics](/admin/analytics) sections.

## Outcomes

How the customer-facing [Agent](/automation/quinn-agent) handled the conversations it joined:

- **Conversations involved**: how many conversations Quinn took part in
- **Resolved**: split into confirmed resolutions (the customer said so) and assumed ones
- **Escalated**: how often Quinn handed off to a human
- **Pending**: conversations Quinn is still working on
- **AI CSAT**: satisfaction ratings on conversations Quinn handled
- **System errors**: turns that failed for a technical reason

## Actions

How often each [action](/automation/actions) was attempted, completed, or failed, broken down per action.

## Copilot

How your team used [Copilot](/automation/quinn-copilot) over the same period. If nobody used it, the section says there was no Copilot activity.

<Tip>
Before you rely on Quinn with real customers, try it yourself in [Messenger](/widget) with the questions your customers actually ask. Pull a few real transcripts and see how Quinn handles them.
</Tip>

# Test and monitor Quinn

Don't find out how Quinn handles a tricky question from an angry customer. Try it yourself first, then keep an eye on how it's actually doing once it's live.

## Test agent sandbox

Find it at **Admin → AI & Automation → Test agent**.

The sandbox runs Quinn against your saved configuration: current knowledge sources, guidance, and actions. Nothing you do here reaches your inbox or a real customer.

Switch between testing the **Agent** (the customer-facing role Quinn plays in Messenger) and **Copilot** (the teammate-facing Q&A role) to see how each responds with the same settings.

<Tip>
Test with the questions your customers actually ask, not idealized ones. Pull a few real transcripts and see how Quinn handles them before you turn on automatic replies.
</Tip>

## Performance

Find it at **Admin → AI & Automation → Performance**.

### Agent metrics

Always visible, covering the customer-facing Agent:

- **Involvement rate** -- how many conversations Quinn takes part in
- **Resolution rate** -- split into confirmed resolutions (the customer said so) and assumed ones
- **Escalation rate** -- how often Quinn hands off to a human, with the handed-off count
- **Actions completed** -- a rolling count, plus a 30-day trend

### Actions funnel

Also always visible: how often each built-in action is attempted, completed, or fails, broken down per action.

### Copilot usage

<Note>
Copilot usage metrics only appear once Inbox AI is on. See [Use Quinn Copilot](/automation/quinn-copilot).
</Note>

Once Inbox AI is on, the Performance page adds:

- Questions asked, transforms run, and summaries generated
- An insert-rate funnel showing how often a Copilot answer, transform, or summary actually lands in a reply or note
- Helpful votes, including how many were marked unhelpful with a reason
- Top teammates and a breakdown of transforms by kind

Two sections appear only when their matching Labs flag is on:

- **Actions funnel** -- approval rate and proposed/approved/rejected/expired counts, once Assistant actions is on
- **Suggestions** -- acceptance rate and shown/inserted/dismissed counts, once Proactive suggested replies is on

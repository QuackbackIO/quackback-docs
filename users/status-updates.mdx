# Follow service status

Before you file a ticket about something being down, check whether it's a known issue. The status page shows what's working, what isn't, and what's scheduled.

## Check current status

Visit your organization's status page, typically at `/status` on their portal. It shows each service's current state and an uptime bar for recent history. Click into an active incident to see the full timeline of updates as the team investigates and resolves it.

## Subscribe to updates

Click **Subscribe** on the status page and sign in. You can subscribe to updates for the whole page, or narrow it to specific services you actually depend on.

Once subscribed, you get an email whenever an incident is reported, updated, or resolved, and whenever maintenance is scheduled on the services you follow.

## Unsubscribe

Sign back into the portal and open the subscribe panel again to turn your subscription off.

## Follow by RSS

If you'd rather not get emails, subscribe to the status page's RSS feed at `/status/feed` in your feed reader instead.

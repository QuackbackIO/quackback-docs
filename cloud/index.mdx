# Managed Cloud

Skip the servers. Quackback Cloud is the hosted edition of the same product, with workspace plans and usage meters built in.

Quackback comes in two editions:

- **Quackback OSS** - fully open-source under AGPL. Run it yourself on any Docker-capable infrastructure. Every feature is included with no limits.
- **Quackback Cloud** - managed hosting on our infrastructure with four plans: Free, Pro, Business, and Enterprise.

The product code is the same. Cloud adds:

- Managed provisioning, billing, and upgrades
- Per-tenant isolation on shared Postgres clusters
- Per-tenant Let's Encrypt certificates and a default subdomain
- Custom-domain support on Pro and above

See [quackback.io/pricing](https://quackback.io/pricing) for current prices and the full feature matrix.

## Plans

| Plan | Seats | Monthly | Annual | AI usage included |
|------|-------|---------|--------|-------------------|
| **Free** | 1 | $0/mo | $0 | $0 |
| **Pro** | 5 | $37/mo | $29/mo ($348/yr) | $10/mo |
| **Business** | 20 | $75/mo | $59/mo ($708/yr) | $30/mo |
| **Enterprise** | Unlimited | $129/mo | $99/mo ($1,188/yr) | $100/mo |

Pro is the recommended plan. Annual billing saves versus monthly: $96/yr on Pro, $192/yr on Business, $360/yr on Enterprise.

Free includes 1 seat, 2 boards, and 50 posts. Paid plans include unlimited boards and posts. Custom domains start at Pro. [Integrations](/integrations/overview) start at Business. [SSO](/auth/sso) and the [audit log](/admin/security#audit-log) are on Enterprise.

## Seats

Cloud bills a flat workspace price. Each plan includes seats. You invite teammates into those seats.

- Every admin-side teammate (Admin or Member) uses one included seat.
- [Portal invites](/admin/users) for end users are free and unlimited.
- Free includes 1 seat. Pro includes 5. Business includes 20. Enterprise is unlimited.
- A pending team invite holds a seat until it is accepted, cancelled, or expires.

When every included seat is in use, [upgrade the plan](/cloud/upgrade-downgrade) to invite more. You cannot buy extra seats on the same plan. Removing a member frees a seat in the meter. It does not change what you pay.

<Tip>
An invite needs an open seat. If every seat is in use, upgrade from **Admin → Settings → Plans & billing**.
</Tip>

Trials use the included seats of the plan you are trialing. Using a Free workspace starts a 14-day Pro trial (5 seats).

## AI usage

All AI features (assistant, drafts, insights, Copilot conversations) ship on every paid plan. Plans differ by monthly allowance, not by which features you get.

When the included allowance runs out, buy a $10 top-up pack. Extra credit carries over month to month until you use it. Included usage is always consumed first.

There is no per-outcome fee and no Copilot add-on.

See [AI features](/cloud/ai-operations) for the allowance table and how top-ups work.

## Plans & billing

Open **Admin → Settings → Plans & billing** to:

- See your current plan, renewal date, and workspace price
- Read the seats meter (members, pending invites, available seats)
- Read usage meters for AI (in dollars), emails, and API requests
- Compare Free, Pro, Business, and Enterprise
- Upgrade now, or schedule a downgrade at period end
- Top up AI ($10 pack) or email ($10 per 10,000 emails)

A **Remove Quackback branding** add-on ($59/mo, or $590/yr) appears on the page. It is display-only until you can buy it.

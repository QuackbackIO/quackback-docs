# Managed Cloud

Skip the servers. Quackback Cloud is the hosted edition of the same product, with plans, seats, and usage meters built in.

Quackback comes in two editions:

- **Quackback OSS** - fully open-source under AGPL. Run it yourself on any Docker-capable infrastructure. Every feature is included with no limits.
- **Quackback Cloud** - managed hosting on our infrastructure with four plans: Free, Growth, Pro, and Scale.

The product code is the same. Cloud adds:

- Managed provisioning, billing, and upgrades
- Per-tenant isolation on shared Postgres clusters
- Per-tenant Let's Encrypt certificates and a default subdomain
- Custom-domain support on Growth and above

See [quackback.io/pricing](https://quackback.io/pricing) for current prices and the full feature matrix.

## Plans

| Plan | Per seat (annual) | Per seat (monthly) | AI usage included |
|------|-------------------|--------------------|-------------------|
| **Free** | $0 (1 seat included) | $0 | $0 |
| **Growth** | $12/mo ($144/seat/yr) | $15 | $10/mo |
| **Pro** | $24/mo ($288/seat/yr) | $30 | $30/mo |
| **Scale** | $49/mo ($588/seat/yr) | $59 | $100/mo |

Annual billing is two months free versus monthly.

Every plan includes unlimited boards and posts. Custom domains start at Growth. [SSO](/auth/sso) and the [audit log](/admin/security#audit-log) are on Scale.

## Seats

Cloud bills per seat. You buy seats, then invite people into them.

- Every admin-side teammate (Admin or Member) uses one purchased seat. Admins and members cost the same.
- [Portal invites](/admin/users) for end users are free and unlimited.
- Free includes 1 seat. The second team invite is blocked until you upgrade.
- A pending team invite holds a seat until it is accepted, cancelled, or expires.

Seats change only when you buy more or schedule a decrease. Removing a member frees a seat in the meter. Your paid quantity stays until you click **Remove seats**.

<Tip>
An invite needs an open seat. If every seat is in use, click **Add a seat** on the invite dialog, or upgrade from Free.
</Tip>

Trials and complimentary grants have uncapped seats. After a trial, checkout defaults the quantity to how many seats you already use.

## AI usage

All AI features (assistant, drafts, insights, Copilot conversations) ship on every paid plan. Plans differ by monthly allowance, not by which features you get.

When the included allowance runs out, buy a $10 top-up pack. Extra credit carries over month to month until you use it. Included usage is always consumed first.

There is no per-outcome fee and no Copilot add-on.

See [AI features](/cloud/ai-operations) for the allowance table and how top-ups work.

## Plans & billing

Open **Admin → Settings → Plans & billing** to:

- See your current plan, renewal date, and per-seat price
- Read the seats meter (members, pending invites, available seats)
- Read usage meters for AI (in dollars), emails, and API requests
- Compare Free, Growth, Pro, and Scale
- Buy seats immediately, or schedule a seat decrease at period end
- Top up AI or email with a $10 pack

A **Remove Quackback branding** add-on ($59/mo, or $590/yr) appears on the page. It is display-only until you can buy it.

## Existing paid workspaces

If you already pay a flat per-workspace price, you keep that price and uncapped seats until you change plan. Changing plan moves you onto per-seat billing with quantity set to the seats you currently use.

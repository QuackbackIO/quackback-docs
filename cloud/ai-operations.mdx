# AI features

Every paid Cloud plan includes the same AI features. You pay for a monthly dollar allowance, not per outcome, and not as a Copilot add-on.

Free includes $0 of AI usage.

## What is included

On Growth, Pro, and Scale you can use:

- **Assistant** - ask questions across your feedback
- **Drafts** - suggested replies and post copy
- **Insights** - summaries, sentiment, and trends
- **Copilot conversations** - in-product help for your team
- **Semantic search** - find related posts even when the wording differs
- **Similar-post and duplicate suggestions** - catch merges early when you triage
- **Post summaries** - the core user need, key quotes, and suggested next steps

Plans differ by how much usage is included, not by which of these features you get.

See [Use AI-powered features](/admin/ai-features) for how summaries, duplicates, and suggestions work in the product.

<Note>
Cloud turns these on for you. Self-hosted installs configure their own AI endpoint. See the [AI guide](/admin/ai-features#configure-ai).
</Note>

## Allowances

Included AI usage per workspace per month:

| Plan | Included |
|------|----------|
| Free | $0 |
| Growth | $10 |
| Pro | $30 |
| Scale | $100 |

The meter on **Admin → Settings → Plans & billing** shows dollars used of dollars included, plus any extra credit beside it.

## Top up

When the included allowance runs out, **Top up** buys a $10 pack.

- Extra credit carries over month to month until you use it.
- Included usage is always consumed first. Extra credit is drawn only after.

Emails work the same way. **Top up** buys $10 per 10,000 emails. Included email usage is consumed first, and extra credit carries over until you use it.

<Tip>
Buy a pack before you hit the limit if a busy week is coming. Credit does not expire at month end.
</Tip>

## What you are not billed for

Cloud does not charge per resolved conversation. There is no Copilot add-on SKU. AI is the included allowance plus optional top-up packs.

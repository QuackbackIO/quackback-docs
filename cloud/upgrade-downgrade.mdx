# Upgrades and downgrades

Change plan from **Admin → Settings → Plans & billing**. Upgrades apply now. Downgrades wait until the end of the billing period.

## Hitting a limit

When you try something that exceeds a limit, Quackback shows the limit, your current usage, and what to do next. Existing data stays. You can keep editing, voting on, and commenting on what you have.

Free includes 2 boards and 50 posts. Paid plans include unlimited boards and posts.

### Boards and posts

On Free, creating a board or post above those caps is blocked until you [upgrade](#change-plan), delete a board, or archive a post. Pro, Business, and Enterprise have no board or post cap.

### Team seats

An invite needs an open seat. Free includes 1 seat, so the second team invite is blocked until you [upgrade](#change-plan). Pro includes 5 seats, Business 20, and Enterprise is unlimited. When every seat is in use, the invite dialog tells you to upgrade.

Pending team invites count toward seats in use. [Portal invites](/admin/users) do not.

### AI, email, and API usage

Usage meters on Plans & billing show included AI (in dollars), emails, and API requests. When AI or email runs out, **Top up** buys a pack: $10 of AI usage, or $10 per 10,000 emails. Included usage is always consumed first. Extra credit carries over until you use it.

See [AI features](/cloud/ai-operations) for included allowances and how credit is consumed.

## Change plan

1. Go to **Admin → Settings → Plans & billing**.
2. Pick monthly or annual billing. Annual saves versus monthly: $96/yr on Pro, $192/yr on Business, $360/yr on Enterprise.
3. Choose Free, Pro, Business, or Enterprise.

Upgrades take effect immediately. New limits land on your workspace within a few seconds. Quackback prorates the upgrade against your current billing cycle.

Converting a trial or checking out from Free is refused if members plus pending invites exceed the plan's included seats. Remove members or choose a plan that covers them.

<Note>
Custom domains start at Pro. [Integrations](/integrations) start at Business. [SSO](/auth/sso) and the [audit log](/admin/security#audit-log) are on Enterprise.
</Note>

## Included seats

Each plan includes a seat cap: Free 1, Pro 5, Business 20, Enterprise unlimited. Upgrading raises the cap immediately. You cannot buy extra seats on the same plan.

Removing a member frees a seat in the meter. It does not change what you pay.

<Warning>
Inviting or removing members does not change your bill. To raise the cap, [upgrade](#change-plan).
</Warning>

## Downgrade

A plan downgrade is scheduled. You keep the current plan until the billing period ends, then the new limits apply.

**Existing data stays accessible.** Boards, posts, members, and integrations keep working.

Members above the new seat cap keep access, but you cannot invite above that cap. Boards and posts above Free's 2-board and 50-post caps stay too. You cannot create more until you are at or below the cap, or you upgrade. Usage meters and Enterprise-only features follow the new plan after the period ends.

Quackback never deletes your data on downgrade.

### Examples

- **You have 6 team members on Business and schedule a downgrade to Pro (5 seats).** Everyone keeps access. After the period ends you cannot invite anyone else until you are at or below 5, or you upgrade.
- **You have 8 boards on Pro and schedule a downgrade to Free (2 boards).** All 8 stay accessible. After the period ends you cannot create another board until you are at or below 2, or you upgrade.
- **You have 200 posts on Pro and schedule a downgrade to Free (50 posts).** Every post stays. After the period ends you cannot create another post until you are at or below 50, or you upgrade.
- **You have used $28 of Business's $30 AI allowance this month and move to Pro ($10 included).** Existing AI output stays. New AI calls draw from Pro's allowance plus any extra credit you already bought.
- **You use [SSO](/auth/sso) on Enterprise and downgrade to Business.** Enterprise-only features (SSO and the audit log) stop being available after the period ends.

## Trials

Each paid plan includes a 14-day trial, once per plan. Start it from Plans & billing, or pick a plan when you create the workspace. Using a Free workspace starts a 14-day Pro trial (5 seats).

Seats during a trial or a complimentary grant follow the serving plan's included cap. At the end of the trial, your workspace returns to Free unless you subscribe.

<Note>
You get one trial per paid plan (Pro, Business, and Enterprise). A Pro trial does not spend your Business or Enterprise trial.
</Note>

# Upgrades and downgrades

Change plan or seat count from **Admin → Settings → Plans & billing**. Upgrades and seat increases apply now. Downgrades and seat decreases wait until the end of the billing period.

## Hitting a limit

When you try something that exceeds a limit, Quackback shows the limit, your current usage, and what to do next. Existing data stays. You can keep editing, voting on, and commenting on what you have.

Boards and posts are unlimited on every plan.

### Team seats

An invite needs an open seat. Free includes 1 seat, so the second team invite is blocked until you [upgrade](#change-plan). On a paid plan, the invite dialog says every seat is in use and offers **Add a seat**.

Pending team invites count toward seats in use. [Portal invites](/admin/users) do not.

### AI, email, and API usage

Usage meters on Plans & billing show included AI (in dollars), emails, and API requests. When AI or email runs out, **Top up** buys a pack: $10 of AI usage, or $10 per 10,000 emails. Included usage is always consumed first. Extra credit carries over until you use it.

See [AI features](/cloud/ai-operations) for included allowances and how credit is consumed.

## Change plan

1. Go to **Admin → Settings → Plans & billing**.
2. Pick monthly or annual billing. Annual is two months free.
3. Choose Free, Growth, Pro, or Scale.

Upgrades take effect immediately. New limits land on your workspace within a few seconds. Quackback prorates the upgrade against your current billing cycle.

A checkout from Free, or converting a trial, defaults the seat quantity to how many seats you already use.

<Note>
Custom domains start at Growth. [SSO](/auth/sso) and the [audit log](/admin/security#audit-log) are on Scale.
</Note>

## Add or remove seats

**Add seats** buys more right away. You are billed a prorated amount for the rest of the period, and the new seats are usable immediately.

**Remove seats** schedules a decrease at the end of the billing period. You cannot drop below seats in use (members plus pending team invites).

Removing a member frees a seat in the meter. It does not change what you pay until you click **Remove seats**.

<Warning>
The roster never changes your bill on its own. Buy the seats you need, then invite into them.
</Warning>

## Downgrade

A plan downgrade is scheduled. You keep the current plan until the billing period ends, then the new limits apply.

**Existing data stays accessible.** Boards, posts, members, and integrations keep working.

Members above the new seat cap keep access, but you cannot invite above that cap. Usage meters and Scale-only features follow the new plan after the period ends.

Quackback never deletes your data on downgrade.

### Examples

- **You have 6 team members on Pro and schedule a downgrade to Free (1 seat).** Everyone keeps access. After the period ends you cannot invite anyone else until you upgrade.
- **You have used $28 of Pro's $30 AI allowance this month and move to Growth ($10 included).** Existing AI output stays. New AI calls draw from Growth's allowance plus any extra credit you already bought.
- **You use [SSO](/auth/sso) on Scale and downgrade to Pro.** Scale-only features (SSO and the audit log) stop being available after the period ends.

## Trials

Each paid plan includes a 7-day trial, once per plan. Start it from Plans & billing.

Seats are uncapped during a trial or a complimentary grant. At the end of the trial, your workspace returns to Free unless you subscribe. Checkout defaults the seat quantity to current usage.

<Note>
You get one trial per paid plan (Growth, Pro, and Scale). A Growth trial does not spend your Pro or Scale trial.
</Note>

## Existing paid workspaces

Workspaces on the old flat per-workspace price keep that price and uncapped seats until they change plan. Changing plan moves them onto per-seat billing with quantity set to current usage.
